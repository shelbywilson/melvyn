Deities in the Hebrew Bible

Milcom (Ammonite: 𐤌𐤋𐤊𐤌, romanized: *Mīlkām; Biblical Hebrew: מִלְכֹּם, romanized: Mīlkōm), also spelled Milkom, was either the national god or an important god of the Ammonites. He is attested in the Hebrew Bible and in archaeological finds from the former territory of Ammon.
